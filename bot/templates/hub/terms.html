the rules

<- back to unixcore.sh

unixcore.sh is free hosting, not a company. by putting a site here you agree to the rules below. break them and the unix team pulls your site and your access.

allowed

  • personal pages, blogs, portfolios, shrines, fan pages, art, writing, projects.
  • anything you made or have the right to put up.
  • weird, ugly, unfinished, under construction forever. all good.

not allowed

  • hate speech. racism, homophobia, transphobia, antisemitism, slurs, or any "these people are less than human" content.
  • harassment. targeting a person, doxxing, revenge porn, brigading.
  • anything illegal.
  • malware, phishing, scams, crypto miners, or anything that messes with visitors' machines.
  • warez, or other people's work passed off as yours.
  • using this as a file dump, CDN, redirect farm, or proxy for something else. host a website.

breaking these rules will lead to a suspension.

questions go in the discord. last touched 2026-09-12.
